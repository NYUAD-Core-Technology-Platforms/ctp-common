# Molecular and Cell Biology

The Molecular and Cell Biology Core Technology Platform provides a broad range of instruments that aid scientists in performing research at the molecular, single-cell, and tissue-culture level.

## Capabilities

- **Gene cloning and manipulation** — cloning and subcloning into a variety of vectors; manipulation that alters specific domains or fusion tags; mutagenesis at single-amino-acid resolution.
- **Protein expression and purification** — efficient E. coli expression for protein samples (including human proteins) at >95% purity.
- **Biophysical and biochemical structural characterization** — characterizing structure, thermal/chemical stability, binding energetics, and screening small-molecule binding partners.
- **Genomics at single-cell resolution** — integrative microfluidic-based system enabling high-throughput gene expression, genotyping, digital PCR, single-cell expression profiling, and sample preparation of single-cell whole-genome or targeted sequencing.
