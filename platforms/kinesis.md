# Kinesis

The Kinesis Core Technology Platform is a space for exploration and experimentation related to motion. The laboratory is divided into two main zones: the **Arena** and the **Workspace**.

**Arena** — the dedicated area for motion experimentation. The Arena is a 17 m × 6.4 m × 8 m space constructed from adaptable truss structures with surrounding nets for safety and can be reconfigured. The lighting system is controllable for colors and intensity, and removable protective mattresses cover the ground area. The Arena is equipped with a local position tracking system, a 2 kW sound system, a projection system, and a high-speed ethernet and wireless network.

**Workspace** — houses workstations for eight scientists with a high-speed local network and controllable lighting system, a high-performance GPU unit, and a safe charging station for LiPo batteries.

## Research areas supported

- Biomechanical gait analysis & rehabilitation
- Posture, balance, and motor control
- Athletics (sports performance, movement evaluation)
- Animal biomechanics and science
- Psychology and neurology research
- Engineering robotics (UAVs, industrial robots, robot localization, movement analysis, trajectory generation)
- Product design and evaluation (automotive, aerospace)
- Virtual / immersive / augmented reality (subject localization, gesture recognition)
- Aerodynamics research
- Control systems, AI/ML (ANNs, deep learning, deep reinforcement learning)
- Entertainment — video-game development (character/object animation, effects)
- Film production — CGI, object/character animation
- Sound and motion applications (music performance and pedagogy, neuroscience, music psychology)
