# Brain Imaging

The Brain Imaging Core Technology Platform performs cutting-edge, non-invasive examination of the brain's structural features and functional activation, and its relation to human behavior, language, and working memory — observed via magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) technology.

The CTP is staffed with an imaging physicist and a certified MRI technologist, and is equipped with a state-of-the-art 3T Siemens MAGNETOM Prisma MRI scanner. The latest pulse sequences (simultaneous multislice for BOLD and diffusion) provide high spatial and temporal resolution with adequate signal-to-noise ratio in shorter scan times. Several RF coils are available with the Prisma — 20, 32, and 64-channel head TIM RF receivers, plus body coils.

The CTP also houses a **magnetoencephalography (MEG) system** managed by the Neuroscience of Language Lab (NeLLab) — a non-invasive imaging technology that allows researchers to investigate how the brain gives rise to various aspects of cognition.

## Main equipment

**MRI**

- Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) 3 Tesla Human Scanner — Magnetom Prisma (Siemens Healthineers)
- Functional Stimulus Equipment for Visual, Auditory, and Response Recordings — Vpixx Technologies
- Infrared Eye Tracking during Functional Examinations — Eyelink (SR Research, MRI-compatible)

**MEG**

- Magnetoencephalography (MEG) — 208-Channel KIT MEG System
- Electroencephalography — 32/64-Channel Brain Products EEG System (MEG-compatible)
- Fast SCAN Laser Scanner
