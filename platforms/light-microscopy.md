# Light Microscopy

The Light Microscopy Core Technology Platform provides access to several state-of-the-art microscopes and supports researchers through experimental design, sample preparation guidance, image acquisition, and post-processing.

## Capabilities

- **Confocal and super-resolution imaging** — applications include molecular-motor transport studies, 3D neuronal network imaging, nanoparticle testing, and cell screening; imaging of fluorescently labelled samples like Drosophila brains, mouse brain sections, and cultured cells.
- **Widefield microscopy** — epifluorescence, phase contrast, and differential interference contrast (DIC) for projects in cell growth and proliferation regulation, actin reprogramming, and C. elegans embryogenesis.
- **Sample preparation** — the facility supports experimental design and suggests labeling techniques and microscope setups for different applications.
- **Data analysis** — various software packages are available on a dedicated processing workstation.

## Main equipment

- Confocal Laser Scanning Microscope (LSM) — SP8 (Leica)
- Super-Resolution Microscope (SRM) — STED 3X (Leica)
- Widefield Microscope — DMI6000 (Leica)
- Stereo Microscope — M205 (Leica)
- Two-Photon Confocal Microscope (2P-LOSM) — FV1000 (Olympus)
- Metallurgical Microscope — LVdia (Nikon)
- Holographic Microscope — 3D Cell Explorer (Nanolive)
