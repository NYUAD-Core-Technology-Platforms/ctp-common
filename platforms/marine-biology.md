# Marine Biology

The Marine Biology Core Technology Platform provides the tools needed for researchers to use the Arabian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman as natural laboratories.

Among the many research activities, scientists at NYUAD examine how coral communities in these extreme environments respond to and recover from mass-bleaching events, and how variation in environmental conditions affects biogeographic patterns of community structure of corals, fish, and other reef biota. Other groups monitor and sample coral colonies around the Emirate of Abu Dhabi to study coral disease, collect plankton for isolation of marine phytoplankton from the Gulf, and explore mangrove sediments for novel marine bacteria with potential for biomedical drugs.
