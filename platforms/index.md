# Platforms index

One-line summary of every Core Technology Platform. Click the title for full description, equipment list, and team contacts.

| Platform | What it does | Key technique(s) |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| [Analytical & Materials Characterization](analytical-materials-characterization.md) | Surface, ultrastructure, and interfacial properties of materials from macro to nanometer scale | SEM, TEM, FIB, AFM, XRD, Raman, thermal analysis |
| [Spectrometry & Spectroscopy](spectrometry-spectroscopy.md) | Separation, characterization, and quantification of chemical compounds and biological mixtures | NMR (incl. DNP), FTICR-MS, Q-TOF LC/MS, GC-MS, ICP-MS, UV-Vis, fluorescence |
| [Microfabrication](microfabrication.md) | Class-1000 cleanroom for material deposition, lithography, and etching | E-beam evaporation, mask aligner, RIE, 3D nano-printing |
| [Molecular & Cell Biology](molecular-cell-biology.md) | Molecular, single-cell, and tissue-culture research; protein purification & characterization | Fluidigm single-cell, qPCR, flow cytometry, calorimetry, BSL-2 tissue culture |
| [Sequencing](sequencing.md) | Rapid large-scale DNA/RNA sequencing for genomes, transcriptomes, and population genetics | Illumina NovaSeq / NextSeq / MiSeq, Sanger, Bioanalyzer |
| [High-Throughput Screening](high-throughput-screening.md) | Automated chemical and biological screening at scale | Robotic-arm-integrated liquid handlers, automated microscopes, incubators |
| [Light Microscopy](light-microscopy.md) | Widefield, confocal, two-photon, and super-resolution imaging | Confocal LSM, STED 3X, 2P, holographic |
| [Brain Imaging](brain-imaging.md) | Non-invasive human brain imaging — structure, function, and electrophysiology | 3T MRI (Magnetom Prisma), 208-ch KIT MEG, EEG, eye-tracking |
| [Marine Biology](marine-biology.md) | Field research in the Arabian Gulf and Gulf of Oman | Research boats, SCUBA, sampling and field logistics |
| [Advanced Manufacturing & Electronics](advanced-manufacturing-electronics.md) | Design and fabrication of custom parts and PCBs, from micro to macro | SLS metal/polymer 3D printing, CNC, CT scanning, EDM, water-jet, PCB plotting |
| [Photonics](photonics.md) | Characterization of optical/electronic devices for high-bit-rate telecom | Tunable lasers, BERTs, lightwave/signal/component analyzers |
| [Kinesis](kinesis.md) | Motion experimentation arena and high-performance workspace | Motion-capture (Vicon), VR, drones, robotic arm (KUKA iiwa) |
| [Veterinary Facility](veterinary-facility.md) | Animal husbandry, welfare, and clinical veterinary care supporting animal-based research | Husbandry, clinical care, facility operations |
