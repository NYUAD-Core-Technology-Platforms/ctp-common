# Spectrometry and Spectroscopy

The Spectrometry and Spectroscopy Core Technology Platform assists researchers in chemical discovery, synthesis, and characterization; protein structure validation; and metabolomics. The facility maintains and operates a wide range of tools for separation and characterization of compounds — supporting the synthesis and discovery of chemical compounds, and the spectroscopic/spectrometric characterization of metabolites in complex biological mixtures.

## Capabilities

- **Chemical discovery, synthesis, and characterization** — separation and characterization tools to support synthesis and discovery of compounds.
- **Protein structure** — NMR and related spectroscopic techniques to characterize and validate large biological molecules.
- **Metabolomics** — quantitative and qualitative characterization of metabolites in biological samples using NMR and mass spectrometry.
