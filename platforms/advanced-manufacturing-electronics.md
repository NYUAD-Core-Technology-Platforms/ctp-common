# Advanced Manufacturing and Electronics

The Advanced Manufacturing and Electronics Core Technology Platform supports research in mechanical design, manufacturing of custom parts, design and manufacturing of printed circuit boards (PCBs), reverse engineering, and the design and manufacture of custom equipment or modifications to existing equipment.

All activities are performed using state-of-the-art software and manufacturing equipment operated by specialized trained personnel. Depending on the nature of the project, a single machine or a combination of machines is used. The facility can produce parts accurate to the micro scale.
