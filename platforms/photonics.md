# Photonics

The Photonics Core Technology Platform is an advanced facility established to address current and future research activities in the field of telecommunications.

Instruments work in synergy to perform advanced characterizations needed to test research theories, and for the evaluation and certification (IEEE standards) of optical and electronic telecommunication devices that operate at very high bit rates. The latest research on data telecommunication channels uses a combination of optical and electrical components to offer data rates of 400 Gigabits/sec. While the combination is essential in telecom, the individual instruments are also useful for research in physics, chemistry, engineering, and computer science.

## Measurements supported

Bit error rate testing · Coherent detection · Vector signal analysis · S-parameter evaluation · Insertion loss (IL) / Polarization dependent loss (PDL) · Spectral measurements · Polarization analysis · Swept-wavelength measurement · Frequency / time domain response · Modulation-format analysis · Fast spectral measurements · Signal integrity · Small-signal and large-signal analysis.
