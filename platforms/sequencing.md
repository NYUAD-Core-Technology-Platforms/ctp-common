# Sequencing

The Sequencing Core Technology Platform enables researchers to rapidly collect large amounts of data on DNA and RNA sequences. Researchers use this technology to sequence samples from humans, model organisms, and non-model organisms to characterize their genomes (DNA), transcriptomes (RNA), and genetic diversity (sequence variants). Other applications include investigating protein–nucleic-acid interactions and analyzing environmental samples.

This core supports projects on marine microbe interactions, neuronal-cell network analysis in mouse models of social-defeat depression, and the **UAE Health Future Study** — the first national cohort study in the UAE, run by the NYUAD Public Health Research Center in collaboration with leading UAE universities and hospitals — sequencing human and oral bacterial samples to study links between human genes, the oral microbiome, and population health.
